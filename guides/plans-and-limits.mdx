title: Plans and limits
description: What each Inkpost plan includes.
---

| | Free | Pro |
|---|---|---|
| Emails | 3,000 per month | 50,000 per month |
| Sending domains | 1 | 10 |
| Price | $0 per month | $25 per month |

The Pro plan costs $25 per month, billed monthly.

The Free plan includes up to 3,000 emails per month.

## Limits on every plan

- Rate limit: 600 requests per minute per API key
- Attachments: up to 10 MB per message
- Logs: kept for 30 days
