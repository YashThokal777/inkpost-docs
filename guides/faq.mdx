## How much does Pro cost?

The Pro plan costs $25 per month.

## How many emails can I send for free?

The Free plan includes up to 3,000 emails per month.

## Which Node.js version does the SDK need?

The SDK requires Node.js 20 or later.

## How long do you keep my logs?

Message logs are kept for 30 days.
